A tiny championship bracket of three robot pickup lines, judged down to one winner. The champion line wins on specifics: a robot at 4% battery, dragging itself in circles, brought back to life by the arrival of its charging cable in another room - longing told entirely in battery percentage and bad navigation.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
